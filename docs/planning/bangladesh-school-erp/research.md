# Bangladesh school ERP market research

Accessed: 31 October 2026. All prices are public observations in BDT, not quotations. Recommendations below are analyst inferences unless explicitly identified as source observations.

## Research method and limits

Reviewed first-party product/price pages from Eduman, EduManage BD, EduManager, EduFusion and Edumi, plus official DPE, BANBEIS, NCTB, DSHE/Teletalk and payment-provider sources. This is a directional sample, selected for Bangladesh relevance and accessible product information; it is not a market-share ranking. Search-visible page content is identified where a direct page fetch failed.

No paid account, live vendor demo, school interview, reference check, penetration test or contract review was performed. Absence of a feature from a public page means **not established**, rather than absent from the product. Advertised features do not prove operational quality. Do not use vendor customer counts as verified adoption evidence.

## Customer segments

| Segment                            | Likely buying concern: hypothesis to validate                                       | Product decision                                                                    |
| ---------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Independent private primary/KG     | Collections, simple register, parent trust, operator workload                       | Simple onboarding; add age-appropriate assessment pack after discovery              |
| Private secondary, Classes 6–10    | Subject/group rules, exams, promotion, fee arrears and reporting                    | Primary initial academic expansion                                                  |
| MPO/non-government secondary       | Staff records, school funds, reporting preparation, existing state workflows        | Export assistance; school payroll must distinguish MPO and institution-paid amounts |
| Larger school groups               | Campus control, consolidated reporting, delegated management                        | Later campus design; same tenant only for legally/operationally shared ownership    |
| English-medium/international       | External examination schemes, terms, subject choices, different parent expectations | Separate curriculum discovery; no claim of support at launch                        |
| Madrasa/technical/coaching/college | Different structures, assessments, boarding or batch/semester rules                 | Separate product packs; not interchangeable with Classes 1–10                       |
| Government institutions            | Procurement, mandated platforms, approvals and public-sector constraints            | Later channel; do not assume direct SaaS purchasing                                 |

School owner/committee is the likely economic buyer, Principal the accountable sponsor, and accountant/operator/class teacher the adoption gatekeepers. These are proposed personas to test. A principal-only demo will miss cashier speed and teacher usability problems.

## Localization requirements: proposed baseline

- Bangla and English names, UI and document templates; language choices independent of curriculum/version. Preserve original spelling and support Unicode search.
- BDT amounts with explicit precision; store calendar dates separately from UTC instants and present times in Asia/Dhaka.
- Configurable weekly closures, Ramadan/day-length schedules, unexpected closures and exam days. Never infer every private school follows one weekly calendar.
- Class, section, shift, language version, group and elective/religion subject enrollment configured per year. Avoid collecting religion unless needed for a defined academic or reporting purpose.
- Cash, bank and verified mobile payments; partial payment, concessions, arrears and printed receipts. Mobile transaction references entered manually remain unverified until reviewed.
- Guardian access on shared phones, multiple children, restricted contacts and non-smartphone communication. A shared phone number must not merge identities or establish custody.
- Reliable A4/thermal output where the school actually uses it; signatures, serial numbers and school-specific marksheet formats after approval.
- CSV/XLSX import/export with Bangla, leading-zero preservation and row-level errors; support schools moving from registers and existing software.
- Connectivity-tolerant attendance with an explicit offline queue only after conflict, revocation and shared-device protections are implemented.

These are product hypotheses informed by the local sources and operating context, not universal legal obligations or confirmed policies.

## Market sizing and commercial research

No defensible public estimate of paid school ERP penetration or Bangladesh ERP revenue was established. Do not invent CAGR, market share or competitor installation numbers.

Proposed sizing procedure:

1. Extract current BANBEIS and DPE institution tables with definitions and academic years.
2. Deduplicate campuses, combined school/college institutions and overlapping primary/secondary reporting.
3. Filter the target curriculum, ownership, geography, size and procurement constraints.
4. Validate operating status, budget and reachable decision-maker using a consented prospect sample.
5. Estimate serviceable annual revenue as qualifying institutions × realistic annual contract value; model acquisition capacity separately.

Illustrative planning scenario only: 1,000 qualified schools × ৳36,000 annual subscription = ৳36,000,000 (৳3.6 crore) annual revenue opportunity before churn, tax, discounts and service costs. This does not assert that 1,000 buyers exist. A capacity-constrained first target of 50 schools at ৳3,000/month would be ৳150,000 MRR; it is a scenario, not a sales forecast.

Validate willingness to pay using complete annual offers, not feature wish lists. Include subscription, migration, training, support, SMS, payment processing, domain, hardware and tax treatment. School-specific quotations and provider agreements are prerequisites to a final price.

## Discovery evidence still needed

Five pilot schools cannot establish national representativeness, but can disprove early assumptions. Include a small primary/KG, a district private secondary, an urban English-version school, an MPO institution and a larger operationally complex school. Interview the Principal, accountant/operator and at least two teachers per school; include guardians with shared-device and low-connectivity needs.

Collect permitted anonymized examples: admission register, external selection list where relevant, annual fee chart, cashbook/day close, scholarship approval, staff salary sheet, assessment calculation and marksheet, transfer certificate, correction request, class routine and survey return. Never put original student records into repository fixtures.

Unresolved: willingness to switch, existing vendor lock-in, payment onboarding availability, support cost, guardian adoption, official export formats, curriculum differences and applicable privacy/retention/tax obligations. Obtain school-policy and qualified legal/accounting review for actual launch documents. This plan does not certify legal compliance.
